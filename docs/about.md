I'm Patrick Morris. I built aftergrid because agents got good at fetching the number before they got good at the work that follows. The answers reaching product and growth decisions were fast and confident. They reported a rate without sizing it against the goal. Some were simply wrong in ways an experienced analyst would catch in a minute, like recommending a rollback because of a traffic shift, or celebrating revenue growth that hid churn.

The skills are free and MIT. Two ways to go further.

## Consulting

Pilot engagements for product, growth, and data teams who want their agents to work like their best analyst: tie the number to the goal, check the story, and make the call. The usual shape:

- **Metric definitions that survive a second analyst.** Numerator, denominator, eligibility, time rules, counter-metrics; written down and reviewed with the owner.
- **Results stated against the goal.** `make-the-call` in the workflow, using your targets and the thresholds that would change a decision.
- **A review step before numbers reach a decision.** `analysis-review` in the workflow, tuned to the failure modes your team hits.
- **Lessons that get retrieved.** A capture-and-retrieval loop so the fourth analyst does not start from the same trap.

Your data stays in your repository. I do not approve your metrics; the owner does. With your consent, each engagement contributes one sanitized observation to the public examples.

Pilots are open. Email me the meeting you are trying to get right.

## Sponsorship

Sponsors fund the public skills, worked examples, and open-data Findings. In return: a named line on this site and in the docs, and early word on what ships next.

Rules that do not bend: nothing commercial appears inside a Finding. No sponsor touches a metric approval or a capability status. Placements live in site and docs chrome only.
