I'm Patrick Morris. I was head of data for ESPN+ as the service grew to 25 million subscribers. Before that I was product lead for the experimentation platform at The New York Times. I work with a small number of heads of data, analytics, and product on the decisions that depend on their numbers.

I built aftergrid because agents got good at fetching a number before they got good at the work that comes after it. An agent can report a rate without sizing it against the goal, or miss something an experienced analyst would catch quickly. The synthetic cases in this repository show two examples: a rollback recommended because of a traffic shift, and revenue growth that hid churn.

The skills are free and MIT licensed. Consulting is how I help directly.

## Background

- **ESPN+ at Disney, 2020 to 2025.** Head of data for ESPN+. I led a 20-person analytics organization as ESPN+ grew to 25 million subscribers. I built the unified metrics framework behind billion-dollar sports rights evaluations, and a self-serve reporting suite that cut ad-hoc requests by 60%.
- **The New York Times, 2013 to 2020.** Product lead for the experimentation platform at The New York Times, used by more than 100 teams. Testing grew from 10 to more than 100 experiments a month, and I introduced dbt for analytics code.
- **Now.** I build AI products on live data. I run Sporting Works, which makes Spot Sports and its AI agent Jerry: a daily briefing that publishes about 290 fact-checked stories each morning for under $3 in model cost. [How Jerry was built](https://www.spotsports.com/blog/building-jerry).

## Consulting

I work with VPs and heads of data, analytics, and product. An engagement usually starts with the decisions your leadership keeps coming back to.

- **Analytics leadership advisory.** I act as a senior sounding board for the person who owns the numbers, on metric strategy, operating model, team design, and hiring.
- **Metrics and decision review.** We choose the metrics that matter and define them so a second analyst gets the same number, including the numerator, denominator, eligibility, time rules, and counter-metrics. Then I review the analyses reaching leadership against the goals they serve.
- **Experimentation programs.** I help design or reset a testing program, including platform choices, guardrail metrics, a review step, and a cadence that can grow from a handful of tests to a hundred a month.
- **Analytics agents.** I help put the aftergrid skills into the agents your team already uses. `make-the-call` states results against your targets, `analysis-review` checks numbers before they reach a decision, and `learn-from-analysis` saves lessons for later analyses to use.

Your data stays in your repository, and your metric owner approves your metrics. With your consent, each engagement contributes one sanitized observation to the public examples.

If this sounds useful, email me about the decision you're working on.
