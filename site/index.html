Your agent can get the number.

04Fieldnotes

The habits
behind the skills.

Four essays on the judgment around the query.

01

The number is not the answer

Getting a metric is close to solved. Knowing whether it matters, and what to do about it, is the job.

↗02

Ask before you query

A clear question names the decision, the denominator, the comparison, and what would overturn the answer.

↗03

The metric moved. Now what?

Separate the arithmetic from the story.

↗04

Analysis that compounds

Keep lessons the next analysis actually retrieves.
