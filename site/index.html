The average changed.
The story needs context.

  • −3.6pp Mix pulled conversion down.
  • +1.0pp Each channel improved.
  • −2.6pp Together they are the total. Not a product cause.

shape-narrative Lead with the decision: do not roll back on this aggregate.

“Both channels got worse.”
Does that claim hold?

  • ↳ Direct: 10% → 11%.
  • ↳ Paid: 4% → 5%.
  • ! An aggregate drop is not a rollback brief.
