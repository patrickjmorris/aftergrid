Open-source analytics skills for your AI agent

Analytics skills for
the AI agent you already use.

Agents are good at pulling a metric. They’re less reliable at sizing a change against the goal and recommending what to do next. I made these skills to teach them that work.

  • −3.6pp Paid grew from 2,000 to 8,000 of 10,000 sessions.

/diagnose-change Separate mix from within-channel change.

Don’t roll back signup yet.
Check the traffic first.

  • −360 Signups lost to the shift: direct fell 6,000 sessions, paid rose 6,000.
  • +100 Signups won back by higher rates in both channels. This is real, but it doesn’t change this week’s priority.
  • → 280 short of a 900 plan. First rule out a tracking relabel, then find where direct went.

/make-the-call Size the drop against the plan and recommend the next move.

01What the skills do

The work before and
after the query.

Pulling a metric is one step. These skills cover what an analyst does around it.

01↗

Frame the decision

“Did onboarding work?” can mean several things. This finds the one the owner is deciding.

grill-question02↗

Explain the change

Reproduces the change and separates a traffic shift from a change within each channel.

diagnose-change03↗

Make the call

Sizes the change against the goal, compares the options, and says what would change the recommendation.

make-the-call04↗

Test the conclusion

Looks for what could be wrong before someone acts on the answer.

analysis-review

You can use the same skills on your own CSV, notebook, or warehouse.
