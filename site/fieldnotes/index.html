A few habits that make the work more useful, whether you are doing it yourself or working with an agent.

01

The useful work before the query

A clear analytical question names the decision, denominator, comparison, and evidence that could overturn the answer.

↗02

The metric moved. Find out what moved with it.

Reconcile the total, separate composition from within-group change, and keep arithmetic explanations distinct from causal ones.

↗03

Make the next analysis start with what you learned

Capture scoped analytical lessons, retrieve them before new work, and revisit them when their assumptions change.
