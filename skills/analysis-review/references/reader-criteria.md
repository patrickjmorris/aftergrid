# Reader criteria

The fallback the Reader reviewer applies when `skills/shape-narrative/references/narrative-criteria.md` is not
installed. That file, when it exists, is the source of truth and this one is not consulted.

Each item is yes or no against the memo as written. A `no` on an item marked **blocking** is a blocking
finding; a `no` on the rest is non-blocking.

## Answer first

1. **blocking** — The Answer is one sentence and it answers the Question. "We cannot tell yet" is an answer.
2. **blocking** — The caveat that would change the decision sits with the Answer, not further down.
3. The Decision it informs names the decision and who owns it.
4. Nothing above the Answer has to be read before the Answer makes sense.

## One Claim at a time

5. **blocking** — Each Evidence subsection makes one point, and its heading is that point as a sentence.
6. **blocking** — Each numeric Claim says who is counted and what the number is a share of.
7. Each numeric Claim says what it is compared with, and over which period.
8. A chart or table title states the Claim rather than naming the axis.
9. Exclusions and limitations are reachable from the Claim they belong to.

## Language the Reader has

10. **blocking** — No term from the profile's `vocabulary.avoid` carries a sentence the Reader must understand.
11. Words from the profile's `vocabulary.use` appear where they fit; the Reader's own words for things are used.
12. Sentences are short enough to read on the device in `reads_on` without re-reading.
13. Numbers are formatted the way a person reads them, not the way a database prints them.

## Strength of statement

14. **blocking** — No sentence states a cause where the Claim type is `descriptive` or `associational`.
    Watch for *because*, *drove*, *led to*, *caused*, *thanks to*, *as a result of*.
15. **blocking** — No sentence implies a trend from one comparison, or a general rule from one population.
16. A difference the Finding calls small is not written in language that makes it sound large, and the reverse.

## Where to go next

17. **blocking** — There is one way to ask a follow-up, and it reaches a named person.
18. The Finding says when it was generated and what period the data covers.
19. What would change our mind is written as something that could actually happen, with a date or a threshold.
