# Portable chart rubric

Use the criteria that bear on the chart; do not invent a numerical quality score.

- **Claim:** Does the title state what the displayed comparison supports, including material uncertainty?
- **Grain and denominator:** Can the reader tell who is counted, over what period, and what a rate is a share of?
- **Encoding:** Are visual distances proportional to the relevant values? Are axes, units, baselines, and transformations visible?
- **Comparison:** Are relevant groups, baseline periods, missing categories, and incomplete intervals shown consistently?
- **Uncertainty:** Are uncertainty or unavailable estimates distinguished from measured zero? Are intervals defined where used?
- **Emphasis:** Does the strongest emphasis support the answer rather than an easier, more dramatic proxy?
- **Reading:** Are labels readable at the intended size, abbreviations explained, and essential series distinguishable without color alone?
- **Evidence:** Can the reader locate the data or calculation? Does the caption separate observation from causal explanation?

For each material issue, cite the visible element and the misreading it permits. If an image was not available, identify judgments that remain unverified.
