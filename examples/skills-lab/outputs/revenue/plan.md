# MRR diagnosis plan
Authored after inspecting the extract, before executing calculations; not preregistration.

Scope: whole supplied five-account population, baseline/current month-end contracted MRR in USD, not cash, profit or recognized revenue. Dates/timezone not recorded. Same conventions and no merges/discount/currency changes are supplied. Founder is decision owner.

Validate unique accounts and finite nonnegative numeric MRR. Reproduce totals and relative growth. Partition account changes into new-at-snapshot (0 to positive), churn (positive to 0), expansion, contraction and unchanged; these mutually exclusive dollar movements must reconcile exactly. Distinguish snapshot entry from first-ever customer acquisition, which is unknown.

Rivals: healthy retained-base growth predicts stable/improving existing-account MRR; entry masking erosion predicts falling existing-account MRR offset by entrants. Compute gross and net MRR retention over baseline MRR, baseline-account loss share, active counts and per-active-account average. If existing MRR grows, erosion is weakened; if it falls despite total growth, headline growth is insufficient evidence of health. No causal source of churn can be tested.

Stop at the descriptive bridge. Cost, lifetime value, tenure, cancellation reasons and intra-month movements are unavailable; they prevent an ROI-optimal acquisition/retention allocation.
