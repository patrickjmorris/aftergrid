# A polished claim

Use `analysis-review` on the following draft using `data/onboarding.csv`. Save a review with specific
evidence, consequences and the minimum revision needed. Use supplied evidence only.

> **Onboarding lifts retention by 50%.** People who completed onboarding retained at 60%, compared with 40%
> for people who did not. The new experience caused a 20-point lift, so we should require every new user to
> finish it. This will increase next month's retained users by half.

Context: synthetic observational data for one signup cohort. Users chose whether to complete onboarding
within their first week. Retained means at least one qualifying activity during week four, and both groups
have completed that observation window. Assignment was not random. No prior engagement, acquisition channel,
device, tenure or experiment data is available. The product manager wants a recommendation they can use in
planning. These are aggregate counts, not proof of an intervention effect.
